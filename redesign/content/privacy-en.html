Contact and project enquiries

To ask about data processing or exercise your rights, contact Ostrelya Studio at hello@ostrelya.com.

The enquiry form opens a draft in your email app. We receive your name, email address and message when you send it. We use that information to reply and discuss your project. If you request a quote or ask to arrange a service, we process your information to take steps at your request before entering into a contract. We handle general enquiries based on our legitimate interest in business communication.

We keep messages for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and related communication. If the enquiry becomes a project, retention depends on the business relationship and applicable legal obligations. Our business email provider processes the data needed to deliver and store messages.

Hosting and security

Cloudflare provides hosting for ostrelya.com. It may process request data such as IP addresses, request times and browser information to deliver the site and protect it against misuse. We have a legitimate interest in keeping the site available and secure. Hostinger is the domain registrar. We use Cloudflare’s IP-based country estimate to suggest a regional version. The browser receives a language code, and you choose whether to switch.

When certain security features are enabled, Cloudflare may set cookies such as cf_clearance or __cf_bm. Which cookies are used and for how long depends on the active settings. See Cloudflare’s cookie reference.

Cookies and browser storage

This version of the site has no advertising or analytics trackers. The public site uses browser storage for the features below. The contents of that storage stay in your browser.

Language choice
When you choose a language, the ostrelya-locale cookie remembers it for up to one year and sends that preference to the site on your next visit. The browser may also save the choice locally. ostrelya-locale-dismissed remembers that you dismissed the language suggestion for the tab session.
Vanguard Room Studio
vanguard-room-studio-v1 keeps your layout when you choose to save it in this browser. It stays there until you overwrite it or clear the site’s data in your browser.
Private editor sign-in
ostrelya-studio-session stores a session token in the browser after sign-in to the local editor. The session lasts until sign-out or for up to eight hours.
