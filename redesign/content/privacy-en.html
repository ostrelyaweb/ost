Hosting and security

Cloudflare provides hosting for ostrelya.com. It may process request data such as IP addresses, request times and browser information to deliver the site and protect it against misuse. We have a legitimate interest in keeping the site available and secure. Hostinger is the domain registrar. We use Cloudflare’s IP-based country estimate for a regional version or language suggestion. The browser receives the country and language codes. You can check the detected country in the footer. With a VPN, this may be the country of the exit IP address.

When certain security features are enabled, Cloudflare may set cookies such as cf_clearance or __cf_bm. Which cookies are used and for how long depends on the active settings. See Cloudflare’s cookie reference.

Cookies and browser storage

This version of the site has no advertising or analytics trackers. The public site uses a language cookie and browser storage for the features described below.

Language choice
When you select a language or choose to stay in English, the ostrelya-locale cookie remembers your choice for up to one year and sends it to the site on your next visit. A copy in local browser storage also keeps the suggestion from appearing again. ostrelya-locale-dismissed remembers when you close the language popup in the current tab. You can clear these preferences below.
Vanguard Room Studio
vanguard-room-studio-v1 keeps your layout when you choose to save it in this browser. It stays there until you overwrite it or clear the site’s data in your browser.
Private editor sign-in
ostrelya-studio-session stores a session token in the browser after sign-in to the local editor. The session lasts until sign-out or for up to eight hours.
