Optional website analytics

If you allow analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits, how visitors find the site, their approximate country and device, and which pages lead to completed enquiries. Google Analytics receives technical browser and network information, page and campaign data, and a pseudonymous browser identifier. The enquiry event records that the server accepted an enquiry. Form contents stay in the enquiry workflow.

Analytics is based on your consent. Your choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. With consent, Google Analytics may set the first-party cookies _ga and _ga_<ID> for up to 180 days. You can change your choice at any time under Cookie settings in the footer. Declining stops analytics on the site and clears its analytics cookies. How Google processes data from partner sites.
